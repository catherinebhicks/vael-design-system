Vael Case Study Slide Library

A pick-and-assemble kit of case-study slide templates in the Vael Blueprint style. Each slide is a fixed 16:9 canvas that exports 1:1 to PowerPoint. Duplicate the ones you need, swap the placeholder text and image slots, and you have a deck. Placeholder content shows NetBase Pro as a worked example.
