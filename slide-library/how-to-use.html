How to use this kit

Every slide is a finished 16:9 template. Duplicate the one you need, replace the placeholder text and image slots, and you have a deck — no layout work required.

Do

  • Duplicate the slide you need and type over the placeholders.
  • Keep one idea per slide — a third bullet often wants its own slide.
  • Use the signal accent for a single point of emphasis per slide.
  • Keep body text large enough to read from the back of the room.

Don't

  • Rebuild a layout from scratch — start from a template every time.
  • Restyle the colors, fonts, or spacing; consistency is the point.
  • Shrink text to cram more on — cut the content instead.
  • Paint the whole slide in the accent color; rare is what makes it work.
