The real constraint

Everything had to run on the live stream

No nightly batch to lean on — every view had to render off a pipeline that refreshed every 90 seconds.

How it reshaped the design

  • Designed loading & partial states into every screen, not as an afterthought
  • Capped the historical range so live queries stayed fast
  • Scoped v1 to the three highest-traffic views; deferred custom widgets
