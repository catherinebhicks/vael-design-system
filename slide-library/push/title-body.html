A clear, single-idea headline

The everyday workhorse: one heading and a paragraph or a short list. Reach for it whenever a slide doesn't need a special layout — most of them don't. Keep the copy to one idea and let the whitespace do the work.

  • Swap this list for a paragraph, or delete it entirely.
  • Three points is plenty; a fourth usually means a second slide.
  • Lead with the takeaway, then support it.
