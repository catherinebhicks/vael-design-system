# PersonaCard

A UX persona summary: avatar, name/role, attributes, and goals / frustrations / trait tags. For research decks and case studies.

## Props

Import from `vael-design-system`.

| Prop | Type / Values | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | `ReactNode` | — | Persona name; initials fall back to the avatar |
| `role` | `ReactNode` | — | Role line under the name |
| `avatar` | `string` | — | Avatar image URL; falls back to initials |
| `attributes` | `ReactNode` | — | Short descriptor line |
| `goals` | `string[]` | — | Goals / needs, as a labeled list |
| `frustrations` | `string[]` | — | Pain points, as a labeled list |
| `tags` | `string[]` | — | Trait tags rendered as chips |
| `sx` | `SxProps<Theme>` | — | Style overrides |

## When to use

- Summarize a research persona on a single scannable card.
- Use `goals` and `frustrations` to frame design decisions in a case study.
- Pair with `ImageSlot` or `LogoWall` in a broader research or marketing layout.
- **Don't** overfill the lists — a few sharp goals and frustrations read better than long lists.

## Accessibility

- The avatar falls back to initials when no image is given; provide a real `name` so initials are meaningful.
- Section labels ('Goals', 'Frustrations') give the lists structure for readers scanning the card.
