# Testimonial

A quote with attribution and an optional headshot. The social-proof sibling of `PullQuote`: use `PullQuote` to emphasise a line of body copy, `Testimonial` to credit a named person.

## Props

Import from `vael-design-system`.

| Prop | Type / Values | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `quote` | `ReactNode` | — | Required. The quote body. |
| `author` | `ReactNode` | — | Required. Attribution name; a string yields initials for the avatar fallback. |
| `role` | `ReactNode` | — | Role / company line under the name. |
| `avatar` | `string` | — | Headshot URL; falls back to author initials. |
| `variant` | `'stacked' \| 'inline'` | `'stacked'` | Avatar under the quote, or beside it. |
| `sx` | `SxProps<Theme>` | — | Style overrides. |

## When to use

- Present customer or user quotes as social proof on marketing pages.
- Use `inline` in tight layouts and `stacked` when the quote should lead.
- **Don't** use it for pull quotes from your own body copy — use `PullQuote` for that.

## Accessibility

- The avatar falls back to author initials when no image is provided; supply meaningful `alt` context via the surrounding attribution.
- Keep quote and author text as real content (not baked into an image) so it's readable and translatable.
