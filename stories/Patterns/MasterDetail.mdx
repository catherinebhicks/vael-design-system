# Master-detail / row expansion

Browse a list of records (master) and inspect one in depth (detail) without
losing your place in the list.

## Live example

Select a row to open its detail in a right-anchored side panel. The list stays
visible, the selected row stays highlighted, and the panel dismisses with the
close control or Escape.

<MasterDetailExample />

The inline-expansion variant keeps the detail in place beneath the selected row.

<MasterDetailInlineExample />

## Two shapes

- **Side panel** — the detail opens in a right-anchored `SidePanel`; the list
  stays visible. Best for quick inspect-and-return on wide screens.
- **Row expansion** — the detail expands inline beneath the selected row
  (`Accordion`-style). Best for comparing a few records in context.

## Rules

- Keep the selected row **highlighted** while its detail is open.
- The detail must be **dismissable** with Escape and an explicit close control.
- On narrow screens, collapse to a full-screen detail (`Drawer`) with a back affordance.
- Don't refetch the list when opening a detail — preserve scroll and selection.

## Build from

`Table`/`List` + `SidePanel` (side) or `Accordion` (inline) · `Descriptions` for the
detail body.
