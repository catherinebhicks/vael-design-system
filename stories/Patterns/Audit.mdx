# Component usage audit

Before changing a shared component, check where it is used across the product. Widely adopted primitives such as Button and Typography appear on many screens, so a small tweak can ripple much further than expected. This audit maps each documented component to its adoption status so you can avoid blind-spot breakage.

_Last audited: 2026-04-09_

## Status legend

- ✅ Active — broadly adopted across the product.
- ⚠️ Limited — used in only a few places.
- ❌ Not adopted — documented but not currently in use (aspirational, or handled a different way).
