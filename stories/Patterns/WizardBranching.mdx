# Wizard with branching

A multi-step flow where the remaining steps depend on earlier answers. The
`Stepper` shows progress; the path adapts.

## Live example

<WizardBranchingExample />

## Rules

- Show the **current** step and total, but don't promise steps you may skip —
  label branch points ("2 of 3–4 depending on plan").
- Keep a **Back** path that restores prior answers (don't wipe on reverse).
- Validate per step; block **Next** until the step is valid, with inline errors.
- Summarize before the final commit (a review step) so branches are auditable.
- Persist progress so a refresh doesn't lose the flow.

## Branch modeling

Treat steps as a graph, not a list: each step declares its `next(answers)`.
The `Stepper` renders the resolved path; hidden branches never appear.

## Build from

`Stepper` · `TextField`/`Checkbox`/`SegmentedControl` per step · `Descriptions` for the
review step · `Button` (Back / Next / Finish).
