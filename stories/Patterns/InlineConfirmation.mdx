# Inline confirmation

Confirm a consequential action without a full modal, by momentarily swapping the
trigger for a confirm/cancel pair (or a small `Popover`).

## When to use

- **Inline swap** — for low-stakes, reversible actions (remove a tag, delete a draft
  row). The button becomes "Confirm? · Cancel" in place.
- **Popover confirm** — for medium-stakes actions where a sentence of context helps.
- **Dialog** — reserve for high-stakes, irreversible actions (delete account, purge data).

## Rules

- Name the **specific consequence** ("Delete 3 items?"), never just "Are you sure?".
- The confirm button carries the **verb** ("Delete"), styled `error` when destructive.
- Default focus goes to **Cancel** for destructive actions.
- Auto-dismiss the inline prompt on blur/Escape — don't trap the user.

## Build from

`Button` (in-place swap) or `Popover` + `Button` · escalate to `Dialog` for irreversible
actions. Confirm success with a `Snackbar` that offers **Undo** where possible.
